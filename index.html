Signs you are ready for a Level 2 charger

A Level 2 charger may be right for you if your car does not fully charge overnight on a regular wall outlet, you find yourself hunting for public chargers during the week, or a second EV is coming home soon.

When a load check comes first

Your panel needs a closer look if your home still has original 100 amp service, your panel has few open breaker spaces, breakers trip when the AC and other big appliances run together, or you already run a pool pump, electric dryer, or shop circuits.

Arizona summers put long, heavy AC loads on a home, and a charger can pull steady power for hours. That is why we measure before we install.

When a panel upgrade comes first

Many East Valley homes can carry a Level 2 charger. Some cannot, especially homes on original 100 amp service or homes already loaded with AC, a dryer and pool equipment.

A Level 2 charger plus AC at the same time is a capacity question, not a guessing game. If the load check shows the panel is short, the honest first step is a panel upgrade, then the charger circuit. We handle both. A typical 200 amp panel upgrade runs $3,500 to $5,900, with Siemens or Square D equipment and a whole home surge protector included on every panel install. See our electrical panel upgrades page for how it works.
